Physics. The science of matter, energy and forces.

Physics explains why things move, how energy is transferred and how waves, electricity and atoms behave, from a bicycle to a satellite.

  • O Level
  • A Level
  • ZIMSEC · Cambridge-aligned

Big ideas

Three ideas that hold it all together.

Master these and every topic in Physics becomes easier to understand and remember.

  1. 01

    Forces change motion

    Newton’s laws link force, mass and acceleration (F = ma). Balanced forces keep the velocity constant; an unbalanced force makes an object accelerate.

  2. 02

    Energy is conserved

    Energy is never created or destroyed, only transferred between stores, although some is always dissipated as heat.

  3. 03

    Waves carry energy

    Sound, light and radio are waves. They transfer energy without transferring matter, and v = fλ links their speed, frequency and wavelength.

Key terms

Words to know by heart.

Examiners reward precise language. Learn each definition word for word.

Velocity
Speed in a stated direction.
Acceleration
The rate of change of velocity, measured in m/s².
Force
A push or a pull, measured in newtons (N).
Power
The rate of doing work or transferring energy, measured in watts (1 W = 1 J/s).
Current
The rate of flow of electric charge, measured in amperes (A).
Resistance
How much a component opposes the current: R = V ÷ I, measured in ohms (Ω).
Refraction
The change in direction of a wave as it enters a new medium, because its speed changes.
Half-life
The time taken for half of the radioactive nuclei in a sample to decay.

Learning by doing.

  • Measuring with rulers, vernier callipers and micrometers
  • Timing the swing of a pendulum
  • Finding the density of solids and liquids
  • Plotting current–voltage graphs
  • Ray diagrams with mirrors and lenses
  • Measuring specific heat capacity

Study smarter

Four habits of top students.

  1. Learn every quantity with its unit and symbol.
  2. Show working: formula, substitution, answer, unit.
  3. Draw graphs with labelled axes and a best-fit line.
  4. Explain answers using physics key words.

Check yourself

Try these, then reveal the answers.

Q1A car travels 150 m in 10 s. What is its average speed?+
Answer

15 m/s (speed = distance ÷ time = 150 ÷ 10).

Q2What is the weight of a 5 kg mass on Earth? (g = 10 N/kg)+
Answer

50 N (W = mg = 5 × 10).

Q3A 12 V supply drives a current of 2 A through a resistor. What is its resistance?+
Answer

6 Ω (R = V ÷ I = 12 ÷ 2).
