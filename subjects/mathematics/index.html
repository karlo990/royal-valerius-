Mathematics. The language of science.

Mathematics trains you to reason clearly, spot patterns and solve problems, and it is the tool that every science and technical subject depends on.

  • O Level
  • A Level
  • ZIMSEC · Cambridge-aligned

Big ideas

Three ideas that hold it all together.

Master these and every topic in Mathematics becomes easier to understand and remember.

  1. 01

    Number and algebra

    Algebra lets one rule describe a whole pattern. Solving an equation means undoing its operations in the right order.

  2. 02

    Shape and space

    Geometry, trigonometry and Pythagoras’ theorem (a² + b² = c²) let us measure lengths and angles we cannot reach.

  3. 03

    Data and chance

    Statistics summarises information, and probability measures how likely an event is, from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain).

Key terms

Words to know by heart.

Examiners reward precise language. Learn each definition word for word.

Integer
A whole number, which can be positive, negative or zero.
Prime number
A whole number greater than 1 with exactly two factors: 1 and itself.
Coefficient
The number in front of a letter in algebra, such as the 3 in 3x.
Gradient
The steepness of a line: the change in y divided by the change in x.
Hypotenuse
The longest side of a right-angled triangle, opposite the right angle.
Mean
The sum of the values divided by the number of values.
Probability
A number from 0 to 1 that measures how likely an event is.
Function
A rule that maps each input to exactly one output.

Study smarter

Four habits of top students.

  1. Practise a little every day rather than a lot once a week.
  2. Learn the formulae and when to use each one.
  3. Always show your working: method marks count.
  4. Check an answer by putting it back into the question.

Check yourself

Try these, then reveal the answers.

Q1Solve 3x + 5 = 20.+
Answer

x = 5 (subtract 5 to get 3x = 15, then divide by 3).

Q2A right-angled triangle has shorter sides of 6 cm and 8 cm. How long is the hypotenuse?+
Answer

10 cm (6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100, and √100 = 10).

Q3Find the mean of 4, 7, 9 and 12.+
Answer

8 (4 + 7 + 9 + 12 = 32, and 32 ÷ 4 = 8).
