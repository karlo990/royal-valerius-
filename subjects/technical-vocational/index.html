Big ideas

Three ideas that hold it all together.

Master these and every topic in Technical & Vocational becomes easier to understand and remember.

  1. 01

    Safety first

    Every practical task starts with the right protective equipment, safe tools and a clean, organised work space.

  2. 02

    Plan, make, check

    Good work follows a cycle: read the drawing, plan the steps, make it accurately, then check it against the specification.

  3. 03

    Skills that earn

    Practical skills lead to apprenticeships, small businesses and further study in engineering, design and hospitality.

Key terms

Words to know by heart.

Examiners reward precise language. Learn each definition word for word.

PPE
Personal protective equipment, such as goggles, gloves and overalls.
Orthographic projection
Drawing an object as separate front, side and plan views.
Isometric drawing
A 3D view in which the receding lines are drawn at 30° to the horizontal.
Tolerance
The acceptable range of size for a finished part.
Specification
A list of requirements that a product must meet.
Prototype
A first working model used to test an idea.
Costing
Working out the cost of materials and labour to set a fair price.
Quality control
Checking that work meets the required standard.

In the workshop

Learning by doing.

  • Producing neat technical drawings
  • Measuring twice, cutting once
  • Using hand and power tools safely
  • Joining by welding, fixing and gluing
  • Following recipes and food hygiene rules
  • Finishing and presenting work

Study smarter

Four habits of top students.

  1. Keep a skills logbook with photos of every piece of work.
  2. Practise drawing the three views of everyday objects.
  3. Clean and return tools at the end of every lesson.
  4. Ask for feedback and improve the next piece.

Check yourself

Try these, then reveal the answers.

Q1Which three views does an orthographic drawing show?+
Answer

The front view, the side (end) view and the plan (top) view.

Q2At what angle are the receding lines in an isometric drawing?+
Answer

30° to the horizontal.

Q3Name two items of PPE needed for welding.+
Answer

Any two of: a welding helmet or mask, leather gloves, a protective apron and closed safety shoes.
