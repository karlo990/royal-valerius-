Our approach

Three words on our crest. One promise to every family.

Our motto is not decoration. It shapes what we teach, how we teach it, and what we expect our students to be able to do when they leave us.

  1. 01

    Science

    Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics taught by specialists, with an emphasis on practical work, clear explanation and disciplined exam technique.

  2. 02

    Innovation

    Computer Science and digital literacy give students the tools to design, build and solve problems — not just answer questions about them.

  3. 03

    Skill

    Technical and vocational subjects aligned to Zimbabwe’s education framework, so every learner graduates with practical, employable capability.

Beyond the classroom

Support that fits each learner.

Strong results come from attention, structure and time. We build all three into the way students live and learn here.

  • Boarding facilities

    Safe, structured and fully supervised residential accommodation, so students can focus on their studies in a calm, distraction-free environment.

  • Private lessons

    One-to-one and small-group tuition tailored to each student’s pace, strengths and examination goals, with flexible scheduling for families.

  • Examination preparation

    Intensive revision programmes, past-paper practice and mock examinations aligned to ZIMSEC and Cambridge syllabi.

Campus life

A campus built for focus.

Open courtyards, quiet reading spaces and well-kept grounds — an environment that tells students their learning matters.

Pathways

Our subject combinations are chosen to open doors to the careers Zimbabwe needs most.

  • Medicine & health sciences

    Biology, Chemistry and Mathematics at A Level prepare students for medicine, pharmacy, nursing and allied health degrees.

    Biology · Chemistry · Maths

  • Engineering

    Physics and Mathematics form the foundation for civil, mechanical, electrical and mining engineering programmes.

    Physics · Maths

  • Computing & technology

    Computer Science and Mathematics lead into software development, data and information systems.

    Computer Science · Maths

  • Skilled trades & enterprise

    Technical and vocational subjects equip students for apprenticeships, polytechnic study and starting their own enterprises.

    Technical & Vocational

About the academy

Rooted in Bikita. Focused on the future.

Royal Crest Academy is a techno-science school in Bikita, Masvingo. We have proudly guided more than eight streams of students through demanding Sciences, Technology and Technical-Vocational curricula.

Our teachers share one mission: helping every student reach their potential. We pair rigorous academic standards with a caring, structured environment.

Admissions 2027

Four simple steps to joining us.

Places — particularly in boarding — are limited each year. We recommend starting the conversation early.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Send us an enquiry below, call, or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll answer your questions about subjects, fees and boarding.

  2. 02

    Visit

    Arrange a visit to see the campus, meet our teachers and get a feel for daily life at the academy.

  3. 03

    Apply

    Complete the application with your child’s most recent school report so we can advise on the right subject combination.

  4. 04

    Join

    Once your place is confirmed, we’ll guide you through uniform, boarding arrangements and the first day of term.
