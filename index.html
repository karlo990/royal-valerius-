Our approach

Three words on our crest. One promise to every family.

Our motto is not decoration. It shapes what we teach, how we teach it, and what we expect our students to be able to do when they leave us.

  1. 01

    Science

    Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics taught by specialists, with an emphasis on practical work, clear explanation and disciplined exam technique.

  2. 02

    Innovation

    Computer Science and digital literacy give students the tools to design, build and solve problems — not just answer questions about them.

  3. 03

    Skill

    Technical and vocational subjects aligned to Zimbabwe’s education framework, so every learner graduates with practical, employable capability.

Academics

Beyond the classroom

Support that fits each learner.

Strong results come from attention, structure and time. We build all three into the way students live and learn here.

  • Boarding facilities

    Safe, structured and fully supervised residential accommodation, so students can focus on their studies in a calm, distraction-free environment.

  • Private lessons

    One-to-one and small-group tuition tailored to each student’s pace, strengths and examination goals, with flexible scheduling for families.

  • Examination preparation

    Intensive revision programmes, past-paper practice and mock examinations aligned to ZIMSEC and Cambridge syllabi.

Campus life

About the academy

Rooted in Bikita. Focused on the future.

Royal Crest Academy is a new private school in Bikita, Masvingo, opening its doors on 1 January 2027. Our curriculum is deliberately balanced: rigorous academic subjects alongside practical, hands-on skills, so every child leaves both knowledgeable and capable.

We promise families a results-oriented curriculum across the board, delivered by specialist teachers in a caring, structured environment.

Admissions 2027

Four simple steps to joining us.

We open on 1 January 2027. Register your child as soon as Grade 7 results are out — before places are full.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Send us an enquiry below, call, or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll answer your questions about subjects, fees and boarding.

  2. 02

    Visit

    Arrange a visit to see the campus, meet our teachers and get a feel for daily life at the academy.

  3. 03

    Register

    Register as soon as Grade 7 results are out. Bring your child’s results and latest school report so we can advise on the right subjects.

  4. 04

    Join

    Once your place is confirmed, we’ll guide you through uniform, boarding arrangements and your child’s first day on 1 January 2027.
