# Zimbabwean University Programmes and Careers from O Level Geography / Weather and Climate

Research method note: official Zimbabwean sites (*.ac.zw, msd.gov.zw / msd.org.zw) could NOT be fetched directly from this research environment (proxy/DNS blocked). Programme names below come from search-engine extracts of official pages (URLs given are the official pages where the extract came from), plus some aggregator sites (flagged). Searched October 2026. Everything should be spot-checked on the official site before printing in a prospectus.

## Q1. Exact degree programme names by university

### Takeaway
Zimbabwe has a direct meteorology degree (UZ, BSc Hons Meteorology & Climate Science), several GIS/earth-observation degrees (UZ, GZU, CUT, MSU Geomatics), geography/environment degrees (MSU, BUSE, GZU, ZOU, LSU), and many agriculture/water degrees. For a Bikita student, Great Zimbabwe University (Masvingo) reportedly has recently introduced GIS/Earth Observation and climate science degrees, but that report comes from an aggregator and still needs checking on the official site.

## Q2. Typical entry requirements (A Level subjects; is Geography required?)

### Takeaway
All Zimbabwean universities require at least 5 O Levels (C or better) including English Language, usually also Maths. Geography at A Level is required for pure Geography degrees (e.g. MSU Geography and Environmental Studies). Geomatics/meteorology-type degrees want Maths and Physics. Agriculture wants Chemistry/Biology, with Geography often accepted as one subject.

### Cited Findings
- MSU Geography and Environmental Studies: "Advanced Level passes in Geography and other two 'A' Level subjects. Ordinary Level Mathematics and English Language are essential." — [MSU programme page](https://ww5.msu.ac.zw/?p=9253)
- MSU general rule (Aug 2026): at least 5 O Level passes at grade C or better including English Language, plus the specified A Level subjects. — [MSU Aug 2026 Intake](https://ww5.msu.ac.zw/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/August_2026_Intake.pdf)
- MSU Geomatics: 5 O Levels including Mathematics, Geography and English, plus 2 A Level passes in Mathematics and Physics. Duration is 4 years per the 2025 advert, though one aggregator says 5. — [MSU Surveying and Geomatics](https://ww5.msu.ac.zw/faculty-of-the-built-environment-art-and-design/surveying-and-geomatics/); [Eduloaded](https://eduloaded.com/midlands-state-university-entry-requirements/)
- MSU Land and Water Resources: 5 O Levels including English, Science and Mathematics, plus two A Level passes. — [MSU Feb 2025 advert](https://ww5.msu.ac.zw/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2025-FEB-INTAKE-ADVERT.pdf)
- LSU Urban and Regional Planning: "5 'O' Level passes including English Language, Geography and Mathematics; and at least 2 'A' Level passes including Geography". — [LSU Aug 2026 advert](https://lsu.ac.zw/public/storage/Aug_2026_Advert_All_Programmes_Consolidated.pdf)
- LSU Soil Science: 2 A Level passes in Chemistry plus any of Biology, Mathematics, Physics, Geography or Agriculture. — [LSU advert](https://lsu.ac.zw/public/storage/Aug_2026_Advert_All_Programmes_Consolidated.pdf)
- CUT Environmental Conservation and Geo-informatics: 5 O Levels including English Language and Mathematics, plus at least 2 A Level passes in relevant subjects. — [Openclass CUT 2025](https://openclass.co.zw/chinhoyi-university-of-technology-march-2025-undergraduate-intake/)
- ZOU Geography and Environmental Studies: 5 O Levels including Geography, English and Maths at grade C or better. — [ZOU](https://www.zou.ac.zw/?p=3891)

### Inferences
- Key message for O Level students: pass English and Maths (C or better). Keep Geography, and take A Level Geography for Geography degrees. Take Maths and Physics at A Level for meteorology, geomatics and surveying. Take Chemistry/Biology plus Geography for agriculture and soil/water.
- O Level Geography itself is explicitly listed for MSU Geomatics, LSU Urban & Regional Planning and ZOU Geography.

### Gaps
- UZ Meteorology & Climate Science entry requirements were not found. They are probably Maths and Physics, but this is unverified.
- GZU-specific requirements were not found.

## Q3. Meteorological Services Department (MSD) of Zimbabwe

### Takeaway
MSD is a government department (Public Service) headquartered in Belvedere, Harare. It is legally mandated to train meteorological personnel and has run its own training school at Belvedere since 1963. It has divisions for Aviation, Agro-met, Public Weather Services, Research and Training. Careers range from weather observer/technician (WMO Class II type) to meteorologist (WMO Class I, degree level).

### Cited Findings
- The Meteorological Services Act (No. 8 of 2003) gives MSD a statutory duty to train meteorological personnel and do research. MSD is headed by the Director of Meteorological Services, whose office forms part of the Public Service. — [WIPO Lex: Meteorological Services Act](https://cdn.nestjs.wipolex.wji.prd.web1.wipo.int/wipolex/es/legislation/details/14555)
- The Belvedere training school was established in Harare in 1963 to train meteorological personnel. MSD restructured in 2013 into divisions for Engineering, ICT, Research, Training, Agro-met, Aviation and Public Weather Services. The head office is at Cnr Hudson/Bishop Gaul Avenue, Belvedere, Harare. — [MSD About us (weatherzw.org.zw)](https://www.weatherzw.org.zw/organization/about-us/)
- Past Finnish Meteorological Institute cooperation trained five WMO Class I meteorologists (one at MSc level), and an instructor was recruited to run a WMO Class II training programme locally. — [FMI Zimbabwe capacity](https://tco.fmi.fi/zimbabwe_capacity.html)
- The MSD director has cited "aged equipment and shortage of personnel". Plans were reported to offer a meteorology degree with a local university, but the article is undated. — [Herald: Weather stations need facelift](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/weather-stations-need-facelift/)
- Zimbabwe Meteorological Society (launched 2013) has student membership and aims at professional accreditation. — [African Farming](https://africanfarming.net/technology/infrastructure/meteorological-society-launched-in-zimbabwe)
- Meteorological technicians collect weather data and operate and maintain the national observation network. This is a generic description from a private college page, not MSD. — [Speciss College](https://www.specisscollege.com/careers/meteorological-technician)
- One anonymous Glassdoor review (undated) says MSD trainees were not paid during training. — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Employee-Review-Meteorological-Services-Department-of-Zimbabwe-E1524488-RVW13375168.htm)
- MSD WMO Permanent Representative listing: [WMO members ZWE](https://community.wmo.int/members/zwe)

### Inferences
- There are two routes. One is the observer/technician route: O/A Level with Maths and Science, then in-house training at the Belvedere school (WMO Meteorological Technician, formerly Class II/III). The other is the meteorologist route: a BSc in Meteorology, or Physics/Maths, then the WMO Basic Instruction Package for Meteorologists (formerly Class I). The UZ BSc Meteorology & Climate Science is presumably the local feeder degree. This structure follows WMO standards, but the Zimbabwe-specific details were not verified.

### Gaps
- No current MSD recruitment notice or entry requirements were found. MSD's website (msd.gov.zw / msd.org.zw / weatherzw.org.zw) was not directly readable.
- The current status of the MSD–university meteorology degree partnership is unconfirmed.

## Q4. Careers and Zimbabwean employers

### Takeaway
Each career maps to a clear Zimbabwean employer. Most public-sector vacancies are advertised through the Public Service Commission e-recruitment portal.

### Cited Findings
- Meteorologist, weather forecaster, weather observer, climatologist: Meteorological Services Department (Public Service). It has an Aviation division serving airports and an Agro-met division serving farmers. — [MSD About us](https://www.weatherzw.org.zw/organization/about-us/)
- Aviation weather: airport services are run by the Airports Company of Zimbabwe (ACZ), whose careers page lists Weather among its services. The Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ) was established on 1 January 1999 to replace the Department of Civil Aviation. Forecasts for aviation come from MSD's Aviation division. — [ACZ careers](https://www.acz.co.zw/careers); [CAAZ Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Aviation_Authority_of_Zimbabwe)
- Agricultural extension officer / agronomist: AGRITEX is an agency of the agriculture ministry, whose name changed in 2026 per Wikipedia. "Agritex Officer" and "Agriculture Extension Officer" roles appear on job boards. — [Wikipedia: Ministry of Lands, Agriculture...](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Lands,_Agriculture,_Fisheries,_Water_and_Rural_Development); [Vacancy Mail](https://vacancymail.co.zw/categories/agriculture-jobs-in-zimbabwe/)
- Hydrologist / water resources: ZINWA operates at least 240 gauging stations monitoring surface runoff and siltation. — [Zimbabwe BTR/5th National Communication to UNFCCC](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/ZIMBABWE%E2%80%99S%20FIRST%20BIENNIAL%20TRANSPARENCY%20AND%20FIFTH%20NATIONAL%20COMMUNICATION%20REPORT%20TO%20THE%20UNITED%20NATIONS%20FRAMEWORK%20%20CONVENTION%20ON%20CLIMATE%20CHANGE.pdf)
- Environmental management officer: the Environmental Management Agency (EMA) is a statutory body responsible for the sustainable use of natural resources and protection of the environment. — [Wikipedia EMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_Management_Agency_in_Zimbabwe)
- Disaster risk management: the Department of Civil Protection coordinates and manages disasters and hazards under the Civil Protection Act. — [World Scientific chapter](https://www.worldscientific.com/doi/10.1142/9789813207950_0024). Related degree: BUSE BSc Hons Disaster Management Sciences. — [BUSE](https://www.buse.ac.zw/faculties/science/disaster-risk-reduction-department/)
- GIS/remote sensing analyst: the Zimbabwe National Geospatial and Space Agency (ZINGSA) works on geospatial and space technologies for socio-economic problems. — [Space in Africa](https://spaceinafrica.com/2021/09/24/zingsa-to-tackle-local-socio-economic-problems-using-geospatial-and-space-technologies/); [Herald](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/sky-is-not-the-limit-for-national-space-agency/)
- Surveyor/cartographer: these are the career outcomes listed for MSU Geomatics. — [MSU Surveying and Geomatics](https://ww5.msu.ac.zw/faculty-of-the-built-environment-art-and-design/surveying-and-geomatics/)
- Geography teacher: BUSE BScEd Geography. — [BUSE](https://www.buse.ac.zw/bachelor-of-science-education-honours-degree-geography-pre-service-4-years/)
- Public-sector vacancies are posted on the Public Service Commission e-recruitment portal. — [PSC E-Recruitment](https://erecruitment.psc.gov.zw/)

### Inferences
- Suggested career → employer map: Meteorologist/Forecaster → MSD. Aviation forecaster → MSD Aviation division at airports run by ACZ, with regulation by CAAZ. Climatologist → MSD Research division, universities, NGOs. Agronomist/Extension officer → AGRITEX, seed and agro companies. Hydrologist → ZINWA. Environmental officer → EMA. Disaster risk officer → Department of Civil Protection (and NGOs/UN agencies). GIS & remote sensing analyst → ZINGSA, Surveyor-General's office, local councils, mining firms. Urban/town planner → local authorities (city/town and rural district councils) and the government physical planning department. Teacher → Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education.
- The planner employers (local authorities, government Department of Physical Planning) and the Surveyor-General come from general knowledge and were not verified in this search.

### Gaps
- No specific vacancy pages were found for ZINWA hydrologists, Civil Protection officers or CAAZ/ACZ weather roles.
- The current exact name of the agriculture ministry (2026 change) should be checked against the government site.
