The design thinking cycle

How it works, step by step.

Innovators solve problems for real people. This five-stage cycle is how teams at our hackathons work.

  1. 1

    Empathise

    Understand the people who have the problem.

  2. 2

    Define

    Write the problem in one clear sentence.

  3. 3

    Ideate

    Brainstorm many ideas, then choose the best.

  4. 4

    Prototype

    Build a quick, simple model of your idea.

  5. 5

    Test

    Try it with real users, learn, and improve it.

Computational thinking

The ideas to master.

Use these words in your answers, projects and presentations.

Decomposition
Breaking a big problem into smaller parts you can solve.
Pattern recognition
Spotting what parts of a problem have in common.
Abstraction
Ignoring unimportant detail to focus on what matters.
Algorithm
Writing clear step-by-step instructions for the solution.
Iteration
Improving a design by repeating the build-and-test cycle.
User
The person your solution is designed for.

Good digital citizenship

Stay safe, work well.

  1. Keep passwords private and strong.
  2. Think before you post: it can stay online for ever.
  3. Respect copyright and credit your sources.
  4. Be kind online, as you would be in person.
  5. Check facts before you share them.
  6. Tell a teacher about anything that worries you online.

Check yourself

Quick questions, tap for answers.

Q1Which stage of design thinking comes straight after ‘Ideate’?+
Answer

Prototype: build a quick model of the chosen idea.

Q2Breaking a big problem into smaller parts is called…+
Answer

Decomposition.

Q3Why do innovators test prototypes with real users?+
Answer

To find out what works and what does not, so they can improve the design.
