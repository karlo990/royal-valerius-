# Gemini prompts: students baking (Bakery & Food Production film)

Short clips for the bakery film, made with Veo in Gemini. Each clip matches a lesson in the film.

**Tips**
- Attach the crest (`studio/assets/brand/crest-640.webp`) and say "use the attached crest exactly, small, on the apron or chef jacket".
- Keep clips 6–8 seconds, slow camera moves, and always say "no talking, no music": our voices and song go on top.
- Ask for "no readable text" so labels and packaging don't come out garbled. We add real text in the film.
- Generate 2–3 versions and keep the one with natural hands (check fingers on piping bags and whisks).
- For the portrait film, ask for "portrait 9:16" instead of "landscape 16:9".
- These are illustrations. Once the bakery is running, replace them with real footage of your students (with consent).

The shared description used in every prompt:

> Black African high-school students aged 13 to 18 at a private secondary school in rural Zimbabwe, wearing white chef jackets with a small navy and gold school crest, navy aprons and white hairnets or chef caps, in a bright, clean school bakery with stainless steel worktops.
