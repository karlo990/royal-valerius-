# Photo prompts: 10 images for the flyers

These 10 prompts cover every photo slot on the six posters (Beyond the Classroom, Our Clubs × 2, Sports, Indoor Games and the catalogue), each in a different uniform and a different part of the school. Paste a prompt into an image generator (Gemini, ChatGPT, Ideogram, Midjourney…), then in the Flyer Studio open any flyer that uses it and click **Upload photo** on that slot.

The studio has a prompt for every one of the 25 clubs, sports and games: click **Copy AI prompt** next to any slot.

**Tips**
- Attach the crest (`studio/assets/brand/crest-640.webp`) as a reference image so the badge on the blazers matches.
- Generate 3–4 versions and keep the most natural one. Check hands, faces and any text in the background.
- These are illustrations. Once the school opens, replace them with real photos of your students (with consent).
- To keep a photo permanently for everyone, save it in `studio/assets/photos/` and list it in `studio/data/photos.json`.

A rugby player running with the ball past a tackler, teammates in support, on the school rugby field on a sunny afternoon.
