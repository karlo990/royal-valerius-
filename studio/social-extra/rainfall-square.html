3 ways to make it rain.

Rain needs rising air. What pushes it up names the rain.
01

Convectional

The sun pushes it up.

Hot ground warms the air. It rises, cools to dew point and builds towering storm clouds.

CLOUDCumulonimbusRAINHeavy afternoon stormsHEREZimbabwe, Nov to Mar
02

Relief

A mountain pushes it up.

Moist wind climbs the windward slope and rains. It sinks down the far side warm and dry.

CLOUDCaps on the hillsRAINWet slope, dry rain shadowHERENyanga, Chimanimani
03

Frontal

Cold air pushes it up.

Warm, light air rides up over cold, dense air at a front and spreads into grey sheets.
