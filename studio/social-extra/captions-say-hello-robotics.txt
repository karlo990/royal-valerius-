=== Say hello to the world (say-hello.html / post-say-hello-*.png) ===

Mhoro! Salibonani! Hello! Bonjour ! Nǐ hǎo! 🌍

At Royal Crest Academy your child learns ChiShona, isiNdebele and English in class, and can say hello to the world in our French and Mandarin language clubs: greetings, songs, games and culture.

🗣️ Did you know? Mandarin is tonal: mā means mother and mǎ means horse.
🇫🇷 French is an official language in 29 countries, and more than half of them are in Africa.

=== Robotics & Coding (robotics-code.html / post-robotics-code-*.png) ===

Build it. Code it. Make it move. 🤖

In the Robotics & Coding club, learners design, build and program real robots: sensors, motors and simple circuits, block and text coding, and team build challenges. Problem-solving you can see working.

💡 Did you know? The word "robot" was first used in Karel Čapek's 1920 play R.U.R. It comes from the Czech word robota, meaning forced labour.
