The rules

  • Hair clean, neat and tidy every day.
  • Boys: short and even. No lines, patterns or designs shaved in.
  • Girls: a short neat cut, cornrows or neat braids, worn back off the face.
  • Long braids tied up in a neat bun.
  • Black or dark brown only. No dyed colours, beads or coloured extensions.
  • Scrunchies and hair ties: plain navy blue only.
