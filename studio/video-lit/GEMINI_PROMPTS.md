# Gemini prompts: "From Page to Podium" (Literature in English + public speaking)

Make each as a **photo (16:9)** and, if you can, an **8-second video clip (16:9)**. Upload the ones you like and
they replace the drawn speaker in the matching scene. Every prompt ends with the same rules on purpose.

**Rules (keep at the end of every prompt):** Photorealistic. Zimbabwean secondary school students aged 13–16 in
navy blue blazers with thin gold trim, white shirts, maroon ties, grey trousers or skirts. Bright, modern classroom
with natural window light. 35 mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin tones. No text, no logos, no badges,
no writing on the board. Camera on a tripod or a slow gentle push-in. Landscape 16:9.

---

## 1. Opening: "Every great speech begins on a page"
A girl stands at the front of the class holding an open book, about to begin a speech. She looks up from the page
towards her classmates with a calm, confident smile. Classmates seated in the foreground, softly out of focus.
*Clip:* she closes the book gently, lifts her head and takes a breath to speak.

## 2. Close reading (used behind the Shakespeare page)
Three students at a wooden desk lean over a printed play script. One highlights a line with a yellow highlighter,
another points at a word, the third writes a note in the margin. Close-up on hands and the page, faces partly visible.
*Clip:* slow push-in as the highlighter moves along the line.

## 3. Habit 1, "Stand tall"
A boy stands at the front of the class, feet shoulder-width apart, shoulders relaxed, hands loosely together,
speaking to the class. Full body visible, low camera angle from the seated audience.
*Clip:* he straightens up, settles his feet and begins speaking.

## 4. Habit 2, "Eye contact"
A girl presenting at the front of the class looks directly at different classmates as she speaks, warm and engaged.
Over-the-shoulder view from behind two seated students, the presenter sharp, the students soft.
*Clip:* her gaze moves slowly from one side of the room to the other while she talks.

## 5. Habit 3, "Pace and pause"
A boy at the front of the class pauses mid-speech with one hand raised slightly, the class silent and attentive,
leaning forward. Dramatic but natural window light.
*Clip:* he speaks, stops for a two-second pause, then smiles and continues.

## 6. Habit 4, "Voice"
A girl delivers a speech with expression and energy, one hand open towards the class, voice clearly projecting to
the back of the room. Camera from the back row, so the whole class is between camera and speaker.
*Clip:* she gestures on a key word and the class reacts with nods.

## 7. "Hook, body, close" (used next to the cue card)
A student holds a small white index cue card with handwriting (not readable), glancing at it once before speaking.
Close-up of the hands and card, the speaker's face in soft focus above.
*Clip:* the student lowers the card and looks up at the audience.

## 8. "Your turn" (practice)
Pairs of students practise short speeches to each other at their desks while a teacher in a smart suit walks between
them, listening and nodding. Lively, warm, focused classroom.
*Clip:* slow dolly along the row of practising pairs.

## 9. Ending: "Read closely. Speak boldly."
A small group of students stands together at the front of the class holding books, smiling proudly at the camera
after their presentations, classmates clapping in the soft foreground.
*Clip:* the group laughs together and one student gives a small bow.

---

**Tips:** if Gemini adds a badge or logo to the blazers, ask "remove all badges and logos from the blazers". If
words appear on the board or the book cover, ask "make the board blank and the covers plain". Keep the same
uniform words in every prompt so all the students match.
