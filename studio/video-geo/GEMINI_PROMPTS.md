# Gemini prompts: weather station, clouds and rain (Geography film)

Clips (Veo in Gemini) and images for *Reading the sky*. Each prompt matches a lesson in the film, and the station details follow the standards from the research report, so the footage teaches the right thing.

**Tips**
- Attach the crest (`studio/assets/brand/crest-640.webp`) and say "use the attached crest exactly, small, on the blazer breast pocket".
- Keep clips 6–8 seconds, slow camera moves, and always say "no talking, no music": our voices and song go on top.
- Ask for "no readable text": we add real labels, numbers and scales in the film.
- **Check the station is right** before you keep a clip: white louvred box on legs, door on the side facing away from the sun, rain gauge low to the ground, wind vane and cup anemometer on a tall mast, short grass, nothing tall close by. Reject clips with the box on a wall, on concrete or under a tree.
- Generate 2–3 versions and keep the one with natural hands and correct instruments.
- For the portrait film, ask for "portrait 9:16" instead of "landscape 16:9".
- These are illustrations. Once your station is built, film the real one with your students (with consent).

The shared description used in the student prompts:

> Black African high-school students aged 13 to 18 at a private secondary school in rural Zimbabwe, wearing navy school blazers with gold trim and the school crest on the breast pocket, light blue shirts, navy and gold striped ties, grey trousers or grey skirts.

The shared description of the station:

> A standard school weather station in an open, fenced enclosure of short green grass, in the Bikita countryside of Masvingo, Zimbabwe, with smooth granite kopjes and msasa trees far in the background: a white louvred wooden Stevenson screen on four legs with its door facing the camera, a copper rain gauge standing low on the grass, and a tall metal mast with a wind vane and a three-cup anemometer at the top. Nothing tall stands close to the instruments.

### C2. Opening the Stevenson screen (lessons 2 and 3)

```
Cinematic close-up, 7 seconds, bright natural light. A Black African student's hand in a navy blazer sleeve with gold trim opens the hinged louvred door of a white wooden Stevenson screen, revealing glass thermometers mounted inside on a wooden frame: a U-shaped maximum-minimum thermometer and two upright thermometers, one with a wet cotton wick dipping into a small water pot. The student leans in and reads them carefully. Shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the thermometers. No talking, no music, no readable numbers or text.
```

### C3. Recording the readings (lesson 3)

```
Cinematic medium shot, 7 seconds, morning light. Two Black African high-school students in navy school blazers with gold trim, light blue shirts and navy and gold striped ties stand beside a white louvred Stevenson screen on short grass; one reads the thermometers aloud while the other writes in a hard-covered weather logbook on a clipboard. Granite kopjes in the soft background. Natural, focused, proud. No talking, no music, no readable text in the book.

A Black African student's hands lift the funnel off a copper rain gauge standing low on short grass, take out the inner collecting bottle and pour the rainwater into a narrow glass measuring cylinder, held at eye level to read it. The camera slowly tilts up the mast to the instruments. Students in navy blazers are small and out of focus at the base. No talking, no music, no readable text.

```
Cinematic aerial drone shot, 8 seconds, hot summer afternoon. The drone starts just above a school weather station on short green grass in rural Zimbabwe (white Stevenson screen, rain gauge, tall mast) and rises smoothly and steadily straight up, the station shrinking below as granite kopjes, msasa woodland and red soil fields come into view; heat shimmer rises from the ground and puffy white cumulus clouds with flat grey bases form overhead as the drone climbs towards them. No talking, no music, no text on screen.

Realistic, majestic, natural colours. No talking, no music, no text.
```
