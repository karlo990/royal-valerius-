Vanishing points

    Why three points? Width and depth lines run to two vanishing points on the horizon (VP1, VP2). When the camera tilts up, vertical lines also converge, to a third point above (VP3). When it tilts down, they converge to a point below. Level the camera and the verticals become parallel again, which is two-point perspective. The sketch shows both cases on one sheet: the top half is drawn looking up and the bottom half looking down, sharing the same horizon.
