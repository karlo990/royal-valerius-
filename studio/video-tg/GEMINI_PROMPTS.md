# Gemini prompts: Technical Graphics at the drawing boards

Images and short clips for the Technical Graphics film and its posts. Each image matches a part of the film plan in
`README.md`. Paste a prompt into Gemini (images), or into Veo in Gemini (clips).

**Tips**
- Attach the crest (`studio/assets/brand/crest-640.webp`) as a reference image so the blazer badge matches. Gemini keeps it closer if you also say "use the attached crest exactly, small, on the breast pocket".
- Image generators garble writing. Every prompt asks for drawings with **lines only, no readable text or numbers**. We add real labels, dimensions and title blocks on top in the film.
- Generate 3–4 versions and keep the most natural one. Check hands holding pencils and set squares, and that the drawings are on the boards, not floating.
- For the round lens in the film, keep the main subject in the centre third. For the portrait film, ask for "portrait 9:16" instead of "landscape 16:9".
- These are illustrations. Once the school opens, replace them with real photos of your students (with consent).

The shared description used in every prompt:

> Photorealistic candid photograph, natural light, documentary style, not a posed stock photo. Black African high-school students aged 13 to 18 at a private secondary school in rural Zimbabwe, wearing navy school blazers with gold trim and the school crest on the breast pocket, light blue shirts, navy and gold striped ties, grey trousers or grey skirts.
