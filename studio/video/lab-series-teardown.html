01 · Colour

Pink October on a school-crest base

Two grounds carry the film: a powder-pink paper for the lab story and a deep crest navy for the parents’ pitch. Gold is reserved for the crest, the dial ticks and anything you can tap (the phone number). Ribbon pink marks emphasis: italic headline words, eyebrows, the Pink October pill.

02 · Type

A literary serif against a tracked geometric sans

The headline face is a transitional book serif whose italic does the emotional work (“in the pink.”, “by drop.”). Everything functional is a wide geometric sans in heavy weights with generous tracking on caps. Closest free matches: Newsreader and Plus Jakarta Sans. Sizes are in the 1280 × 720 frame.

03 · Layout

One column, one dial

Every content scene uses the same split. The left 40% is a copy column that starts at y 166 and ends at the CTA bar. The right side holds a single circular “lens” that never moves; only its media, badge and satellites change. Persistent chrome sits on a 46 / 28 px safe frame. Use Show grid on the player to see it measured.

  1. Copy column: x 67 → 573. Eyebrow → headline → body → device (chips, scale, table), with a 14 px rhythm.
  2. Dial: centre (893, 360). Media disc r 255 with a 3 px gold rim and a 7 px translucent white halo. Gold tick ring at r 262–280 (major every 6th tick). Ring text on r 282 in 13.5 px tracked caps, rotating about 3° per second.
  3. Satellites sit on the dial’s rim at fixed clock positions. The LAB badge sits at 3 o’clock. The inset bubble or burette readout sits at about 7 o’clock, the meniscus diagram at 5 o’clock.
  4. CTA bar at the bottom of the copy column on every scene except the cold open and the outro. On the navy scene it flips to white.

05 · Beat sheet

A titration as a five-step story, then the pitch

The film borrows the structure of a lab practical: set-up, accuracy, control, end point, results. Each step is a numbered eyebrow, a two-tone headline and one teaching device. It then pivots to Grade 7 parents and ends on the crest.

06 · Motion

How it moves

  1. Cold open: a full-bleed macro of the microscope stage, then a dissolve into the eyepiece view while “Look closer.” fades up in italic serif.
  2. Iris into the lens: the eyepiece circle becomes the dial’s media disc. Paper pink fades in around it and the ring and ticks draw on. After this the lens never moves.
  3. Scene changes: about 0.8 s cross-dissolves on the whole frame. Inside the lens each still gets a slow push-in from 1.02 to 1.10 scale.
  4. Copy build: eyebrow, headline, body and device rise about 14 px and fade in, staggered about 140 ms apart. Chips arrive one at a time; the first scene shows one chip, the held frame shows all four.
  5. Data animates: the burette readout counts 23.80 → 24.10 cm³, the end-point pin slides along the pH strip to “pale pink”, and the results table fills row by row before the concordant mean appears.
  6. Ambient: soft pink bokeh drifts behind everything, the ring rotates slowly and continuously, and the frame has a light grain. The theme flips to navy for the parents’ scene, then washes white into the crest outro.
