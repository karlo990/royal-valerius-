11Food & drink

吃 chī · eat

🍚 HSK 1 · Food: 吃 (chī) = eat

🗣 1st tone, high and flat.
✍️ 6 strokes: 口 (mouth) sits on the left, because you eat with your mouth!
📖 吃饭 chī fàn = have a meal · 米饭 rice · 面条 noodles · 水果 fruit · 喝茶 drink tea
