01/20Newton's 1st law

Your body
keeps going.

⚛️ Physics in 20 · 01/20: Your body keeps going.

Brake hard and you lurch forward. That's inertia. Things keep doing what they're doing until a force changes it.

📐 ΣF = 0 → v stays the same
🔎 Example: The car stops. Your body doesn't, until the seatbelt pulls.
💡 Did you know? Seatbelts and airbags exist because of inertia.

02/20Newton's 2nd law

Force makes
speed change.

⚛️ Physics in 20 · 02/20: Force makes speed change.

Push harder, go faster. Add mass, go slower. Acceleration grows with force and shrinks with mass.

📐 F = m × a
🔎 Example: 1 200 kg car braking at 8 m/s² needs 9 600 N.
💡 Did you know? Heavy lorries need much longer to stop than cars.

03/20Newton's 3rd law

Every action has
a reaction.

⚛️ Physics in 20 · 03/20: Every action has a reaction.

Forces always come in pairs. Equal size, opposite direction, acting on different objects.

📐 F(A on B) = −F(B on A)
🔎 Example: Push a wall: it pushes you back just as hard.
💡 Did you know? When you walk, the ground pushes you forwards.

Gravity bends

⚛️ Physics in 20 · 05/20: Gravity bends every path.

📐 y = v·sinθ·t − ½gt²

07/20Gravity

Everything falls

⚛️ Physics in 20 · 07/20: Everything falls together.

Without air, a hammer and a feather drop as one. Gravity speeds every object up by the same amount.

📐 W = m × g
🔎 Example: On Earth g ≈ 9.8 m/s². On the Moon g ≈ 1.6 m/s².
